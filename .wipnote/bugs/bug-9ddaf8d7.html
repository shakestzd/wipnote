The file-overlap, research and commit guards (core/hooks/pretooluse.go, commit_research_guard.go, yolo_guard.go) embed the WIPNOTE_GUARDS_OFF=1 emergency override in the same block message an agent reads, and the kill switch in PreToolUse is honoured silently with no log, agent_event or work-item note.
